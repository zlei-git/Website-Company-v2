# NordicHome Furniture
## Design System & UI Guidelines

## 1. Brand Identity

Brand Name:
NordicHome Furniture

Industry:
Furniture & Home Living

Positioning:
Premium Scandinavian furniture for modern living.

Brand Personality:
- Minimal
- Elegant
- Functional
- Warm
- Modern
- Reliable

Main Tagline:
Furniture Designed for Better Living

Brand Concept:
NordicHome Furniture adalah brand furniture bergaya Scandinavian yang mengutamakan desain minimalis, kenyamanan, kualitas material, dan fungsi.

---

# 2. Overall Design Direction

Website harus terasa seperti website perusahaan furniture internasional yang profesional.

Primary visual characteristics:
- Scandinavian
- Minimal
- Editorial
- Premium
- Modern
- Warm
- Natural
- Spacious

Gunakan photography sebagai elemen visual utama.
Gunakan whitespace yang cukup.
Gunakan typography sebagai bagian penting dari identitas brand.
Jangan membuat halaman terlihat seperti template dashboard atau template e-commerce generik.

Avoid:
- Neon colors
- Excessive gradients
- Glassmorphism
- Excessive shadows
- Excessive rounded cards
- Huge pill-shaped UI
- Excessive animations
- Futuristic effects
- Overly saturated colors
- Crowded layouts
- Repetitive card layouts
- Generic AI-looking interface
